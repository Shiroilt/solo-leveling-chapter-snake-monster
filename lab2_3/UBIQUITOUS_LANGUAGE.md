## Flagged Ambiguities

| Ambiguity | Locations | Recommended Resolution |
|-----------|----------|----------------------|
| **"tail" used for two concepts** | `clearTail()` at `snake0/Snake.cpp:102` erases only the *rearmost segment*, but in natural language "tail" sometimes refers to the *entire body excluding the head*. | Reserve "tail" for the rearmost segment only. Use "body" for the full segment list. |
| **"level" overloaded** | In `GameBoard`, "level" means the derived difficulty tier. In `GameManager`, "level" is occasionally used informally in output strings to mean the game session. The `showLevelProgressionInfo()` function at `snake0/GameManager.cpp:26` uses it to mean both. | Use "level" only for the score-derived tier. Use "session" or "game" for the current play. |
| **"score" refers to player 1 only by convention** | `int score` is player 1's score; `int score2` is player 2's. The naming pattern implies there is a canonical "the score" (P1's) and a derivative one (P2's). This reflects the Shotgun Surgery smell. | After the refactor: use `scores[0]` and `scores[1]`, making the convention explicit. |
| **Single-file vs. multi-file codebase** | `snake/test.cpp` (single-file Raylib prototype) uses `Food`, `Snake`, `eid`, `go`, `sc`, `run`, `lup`, `cellcount` — a completely different vocabulary from `snake0/`. For example, `sc` for score vs. `score`; `run` for game-active flag (unnamed in `snake0/`). | The `snake0/` vocabulary is the canonical domain language. `snake/test.cpp` is a prototype and its shorthand names should not be adopted. |
